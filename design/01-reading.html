4.2 Ablations

We ablate the routing module by replacing the learned gate with a fixed top-k selection. The resulting drop is small, which suggests the gate contributes less than the sparsity pattern itself. Table 3 reports the comparison against the strongest baseline we were able to reproduce.

Our model outperforms the baseline by a clear margin on all three datasets. We therefore conclude that routing, rather than capacity, accounts for most of the improvement reported in Section 4.1.

All models are trained with the same schedule and evaluated once on the held-out split. Additional configuration details are deferred to Appendix B.
